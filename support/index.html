Support — Pesëkatësh

Last updated 25 September 2026

Pesëkatësh is a traditional Albanian card game for four players. Each match is ten rounds: five of Minuses, where you avoid penalty cards, and five of Sevens, where you race to empty your hand.

Contact

Email peskac.support@gmail.com, in English or Albanian. Say which phone you use and, for anything about online play, your username. Authorities can reach us at the same address.

Common questions

How do I play?

Open How to play from the home screen. It explains both games.

Do I need an account?

Not to play against the computer: that is the whole game, and it needs nothing. Online play uses an account, and is for players 16 and over, but it can be a guest account: when you first open the app, tap Play as guest at the top of the page. A guest account lives on that phone. To keep your username and rating when you change phones, use the buttons under Sign in on the same page, or save the account later from your profile.

I signed in with Apple or Google. How do I get back in on a new phone?

Choose the same one again under Sign in: on the page with Play as guest that a new install shows, or under Profile → Save account → Sign in. Those accounts have no password in Pesëkatësh, so an email and password does not apply to them.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address on the account and we will help you back in.

Am I playing real people in ranked?

Ranked looks for other people first. When not enough people are searching to fill a table, the empty seats go to computer players after a few seconds, so you are never left waiting. Tap a name at the table to see which: a computer player's menu says Computer player. The match counts for your rating either way.

How do I report or block a player?

Tap the ⋯ beside their name: in friend search, friend requests, your friends list, the ladder, a table lobby, or at the table. Reporting blocks them as well, and a person reviews every report within 24 hours. You can unblock players from Online → Friends. What is and is not allowed is in the terms and community rules.

What if a player seems to be under 16?

Online play is for players 16 and over. Report them from the ⋯ beside their name and pick Seems to be under 16. A person reviews it within 24 hours, and if the player does seem to be under 16, their account is deleted, as the privacy policy describes. A parent or guardian can also email us to have their child's account deleted.

How do I report illegal content if I have no account?

Email peskac.support@gmail.com with the subject Illegal content report. Please include:

  • the exact username, and where you saw it (the ladder, a table, friend search);
  • why you believe it is illegal, and which law, if you know;
  • your name and email address;
  • a sentence saying that you believe, in good faith, that the report is accurate and complete.

We confirm we have it, decide what to do, and write to tell you what we decided.

My username was reset, or my account was removed

That happens when a username or a player's behaviour breaks the terms and community rules. If you think it was a mistake, email us with the subject Appeal, your username (or the one you had), and why. A person looks at it again and replies.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Save account (or Your account, once it is saved) → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also email us to ask.

Why is online play off for me?

Online play, meaning accounts, friends, invitations, the daily table and the ladder, is for players 16 and over. When the app was first opened it asked for a date of birth, and kept only whether online play is open, never the date. Everyone can play the whole game against the computer. If you think something is wrong, email us and we will help.

How do I make a privacy complaint?

Email peskac.support@gmail.com with the subject Privacy complaint, or tap Privacy complaint beside Delete my account in the app. We acknowledge every complaint within 30 days, look into it, and tell you the outcome. The privacy policy says what else you can ask for, and where else you can complain.

Can I buy coins?

No. There are no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent on card backs and tables in the game. They cannot be sold, traded or given to another player either.
