Support — Pesëkatësh

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Pesëkatësh is a traditional Albanian card game for four players. Each match is ten rounds: five of Minuses, where you avoid penalty cards, and five of Sevens, where you race to empty your hand.

Contact

Email peskac.support@gmail.com. Say which phone you use and, for anything about online play, your username.

Common questions

How do I play?

Open Rules from the home screen. It explains both games.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play every mode as a guest. An account only lets you keep your username and rating when you change phones.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address on the account and we will help you back in.

How do I report or block a player?

Tap the ⋯ beside their name — in friend search, friend requests, your friends list, the ladder, a table lobby, or at the table. Reporting blocks them as well. You can unblock players from Online → Friends.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Save account (or Your account, once it is saved) → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also email us to ask.

Why is online play off for me?

When you first opened the app it asked your date of birth. Online play, meaning accounts, friends, invitations, the daily table and the ladder, is for players 16 and over; everyone can play the whole game against the bots. Only the outcome is kept, never the date. If you mistyped it, open Settings → Online play → Change and answer again; after an answer that closed online play, the app waits a day before asking again.

Can I buy coins?

No. There are no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent on card backs and tables in the game.
