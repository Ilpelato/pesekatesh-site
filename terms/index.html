Terms and community rules — Pesëkatësh

Last updated 25 September 2026

These are the rules for playing Pesëkatësh online. You agree to them when you make an online account, guest accounts included. The offline game, every match against the computer on your own phone, needs no account, and these terms ask nothing of it.

Who can play

  • The offline game is for everyone, of any age.
  • Online accounts are for people 16 and over. Online play means an account: a username other players can see, friends, invitations, private tables, ranked matches, the ladder and the daily table.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it, as the privacy policy describes under Deleting your account.

Your account

  • An account is yours alone. Do not sell, buy, lend, share or trade it.
  • Do not use a second account to play ranked against yourself, or to come back after an account was removed.
  • Keep your password to yourself. What is done with your account is your responsibility.
  • You can delete your account at any time (see below).

Usernames

Your username is the one thing you write that other players read, so it has rules:

  • 3 to 16 letters, numbers or underscores.
  • Nothing offensive, in any language, spelled out or disguised: no slurs, insults, hate, sexual words, threats or drugs.
  • Do not pretend to be someone else: another player, a real person, or us ("admin", "moderator", "support" and names like them are refused).
  • No personal details, yours or anyone else's: no full real name, phone number, address or social media handle. We recommend not using your real name at all.
  • No advertising and no web addresses.

How to behave

We have zero tolerance for offensive usernames and for abuse of other players. That includes:

  • harassing, threatening or insulting anyone, or attacking them for their origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or anything else;
  • cheating: working with another player at the same table, using more than one account, or anything else that fixes a result;
  • leaving ranked matches on purpose to spoil them for the other three;
  • using the game to break the law.

How the rules are enforced

  • The filter. Every username is checked by our server against a list of offensive words when it is chosen, and refused if it matches. A filter catches a lot, not everything, which is why there are reports.
  • Reports. Anyone can report a player from the ⋯ beside their name. Reporting blocks them as well. A person reviews every report within 24 hours.
  • What happens next. An offensive username is reset to a neutral one. Abuse, or breaking the rules again, gets the account removed; serious abuse can get it removed the first time. A player whose account was removed may not come back with another one.
  • Being told. If your account has an email address, we write to tell you what we did and why.

Appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong, whether a username reset or an account removed, write to peskac.support@gmail.com with the subject Appeal. Give your username (or the one you had) and tell us why. A person looks at the decision again and replies. A removed account cannot be brought back, because removal deletes it, but if the removal was wrong you are welcome to make a new one.

Coins

  • Coins are earned by playing and from the free daily wheel. They are part of the game and worth nothing outside it.
  • Coins are never sold. They cannot be bought, sold, traded, gifted, transferred or cashed out, and nothing can be staked or bet with them, on a spin or at a table.
  • Accounts cannot be sold or traded either, with or without coins.

Computer players in ranked

Ranked looks for other people first. If nobody joins within 12 seconds, computer players take the empty seats, so nobody is left waiting; the ranked screen says so before every search. The match counts toward the rating of every person at the table. Computer players are never on the ladder, and tapping one's name at the table says Computer player. They play by fixed rules written by hand, not by a trained model, at a level set by the ratings of the people at the table.

At a private table the host can add computer players too. Private tables are never rated.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Save account (or Your account, once it is saved) → Delete my account. It goes at once and for good, with your username, rating, friends and invitations. What is kept, and why, is in the privacy policy.

Online play itself

Pesëkatësh is free. Online play depends on a server, so it can be unavailable at times, and we can change online features or switch them off. The offline game does not depend on any of it.

Changes to these terms

When these terms change, the date at the top changes with it. If a change matters to online accounts, we say so in the app before it takes effect. If you do not agree to a change, you can delete your account; the offline game stays yours.

Contact

Pesëkatësh is made by Evelina Simoni, in Italy. Write to peskac.support@gmail.com, in English or Albanian. Help with the game is on the support page, and how we handle data is in the privacy policy.
